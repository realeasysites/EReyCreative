How I see a space

Details you notice, even if you can't say why

Straight lines
Verticals stay vertical and horizons stay level. Rooms look like they feel in person.
Honest light
I shoot with the natural light of the space and edit with restraint, so what you see is what it feels like to be there.
Composition with intent
Every frame is planned around how people move through, and live in, a room.
